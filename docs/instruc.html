No cart to route yet
Either HEBPath isn’t set up on your phone yet, or you haven’t shared a cart to it. Here’s the whole thing. Steps 1 and 2 you do once; step 3 is the one you repeat.
1

Turn on “Allow Running Scripts”

The shortcut can’t read your cart without this turned on.

  1. Open the Settings app on your iPhone.
  2. Scroll all the way down until you find Apps.
  3. Scroll down to the Shortcuts app.
  4. Scroll down and tap Advanced.
  5. Turn on Allow Running Scripts.
2

Add the shortcut

This is the part that sends your cart over to HEBPath.

Add shortcut
3

Share your cart to it

You’ll do this every time you want a new route.

  1. Open your H-E-B app and go to your Cart.
  2. You may have delivery or the wrong location selected. To change this:
    1. Tap Choose Pickup Time.
    2. Tap Change Store.
    3. Select your preferred store from the list.
  3. Tap the ⋯ (three dots) menu.
  4. Tap Share.
  5. In the share sheet, scroll down until you see HEBPath, then tap it.

Don’t see it?Scroll to the very bottom of the share sheet, tap Edit Actions…, and switch HEBPath on.

You can get back here anytime from the setup link on the map. Back to the map.
